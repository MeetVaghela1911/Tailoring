# Privacy Policy for Tailoring App

**App Name:** Tailoring App

## Introduction

At Tailoring App, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you provide while using our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our app:

- **Personal Information:**
  - **Email Address:** Required for user authentication.
  - **Password/Authentication Data:** For secure account access.
- **Location Information:** We may access your location data when using specific features of the app to provide location-based context for orders or tracking.
- **Images and Device Storage:** We collect images when you choose to upload garment photos, measurements, or references using the camera or gallery.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To manage user accounts and provide access to the app's features.
- To store relevant records (like item photos and details) associated with your tailoring business processes.
- To enhance security and authenticate users.

## 3. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and have implemented industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Your data is stored on secure servers.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties. All collected data is used exclusively within the app for internal purposes and to provide the intended service.

## 5. User Rights

As a user, you have the right to access, modify, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us via email at info.dev.meet@gmail.com.

## 6. Account Deletion

If you choose to delete your account, all your data will be deleted from our system within 90 working days. Please note that after deletion, all your information, including records and any other personal data, will be permanently removed.

## 7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please contact us at: 
**Email:** info.dev.meet@gmail.com
