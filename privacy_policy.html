Privacy Policy for Tailoring App

App Name: Tailoring App

Introduction

At Tailoring App, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you provide while using our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our app:

  • Personal Information:
    • Email Address: Required for user authentication and account management.
    • Password/Authentication Information: For secure account access.
  • Location Information: We may access your location data only when using specific features of the app that require location tracking or tagging.
  • Images and Photos: If you use the app to upload photos of garments, measurements, or receipts, these images will be collected and stored to provide the required functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To manage user accounts and provide access to the app's tailoring and order management features.
  • To store and process images and details related to orders.
  • To enhance security and authenticate users.

3. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and have implemented industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Your data is stored on secure cloud servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties. All collected data is used exclusively within the Tailoring App for providing our services.

5. User Rights

As a user, you have the right to access, modify, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us via email at info.dev.meet@gmail.com.

6. Account Deletion

If you choose to delete your account, all your personal data will be deleted from our system within a reasonable timeframe (typically 90 days). Please note that after deletion, all your information, including saved orders and any other personal data, will be permanently removed.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please contact us at:
